About CCS Line

A Decade of Trust on the High Seas.

CCS Line is based in Dubai (UAE) and started operations in 2014. We connect businesses to global trade routes through dependable container shipping, full-load and consolidated sea freight services.

Our commitment is simple — move your cargo safely, transparently, and on time. With a network of trusted partners across major ports, we make global shipping feel local.
